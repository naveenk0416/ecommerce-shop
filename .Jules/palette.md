## 2025-05-10 - Improved Accessibility of Icon-Only Buttons\n**Learning:** Icon-only buttons lacking accessible names and screen-reader-hidden icons degrade UX for assistive technology users. Providing `aria-label`, `title`, and `aria-hidden='true'` on icons within buttons is a high-impact micro-UX win that meets accessibility requirements.\n**Action:** Applied these attributes to `btn-icon-premium` and `btn-icon-dark` buttons in app shell and product listing views.
